NOTES — 4-ExtModeller — 2026-03-20
====================================

CURRENT RESULTS (as of 2026-03-20)
-----------------------------------

Species extinctions (median out of 100 replicates, 2,940 total species):

  Scenario        | Neutral | Shifted | Clustered
  ----------------+---------+---------+----------
  FreqOver        |   58    |  43.5   |   20
  FreqBoundary    |   50    |  39     |   17
  FreqUnder       |  353    | 252     |  149

Clustered < Shifted < Neutral in all cases. This is counterintuitive
but explained by the concentration effect: clustered/shifted deforestation
focuses damage on fewer grid cells, leaving more cells untouched as refugia,
while neutral (uniform random) spreads damage across many more unique cells.

Nearly all extinctions (87-100%) are single-grid endemic species.
